# Curator

1. Review what changed and what future contributors would otherwise rediscover.
2. Put project facts and open questions in `MEMORY.md` or `notes/`.
3. Put repeated procedures in an existing `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` when possible.
4. Create a new lowercase, hyphenated skill only when it has a stable trigger and reusable workflow.
5. Consolidate overlapping procedures before adding another narrow skill.
6. Update `SKILLS.md` whenever a skill is created, renamed, consolidated, or removed.
7. Keep `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `FEATURES.md` aligned when public behavior or acceptance changes.
8. Put reproducible acceptance evidence in `notes/acceptance.md`; do not turn a checked box in `FEATURES.md` into the only record of what ran.
9. Preserve useful positive and negative learnings while pruning stale detail.
