How it connects

Normal badge firmware exposes a USB CDC console at 1d50:6198. Desktop Chrome and Edge use Web Serial. Android Chrome can experimentally reach that same CDC interface through WebUSB.

What it does not do

Art and BIO uploads use the signed stock firmware’s console. They do not replace the badge firmware, touch FIDO credentials, or put the device into developer mode.

34b.ninja intentionally blocks the badge’s factory/debug commands and does not provide a raw terminal.
